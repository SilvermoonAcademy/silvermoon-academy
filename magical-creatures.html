CREATURES OF THE FOUR HOUSES

Guardians of the Founders' Legacy

Each House of Silvermoon carries the legacy of one of the academy's founders. Over time, four magical creatures became symbols of their values, personalities and histories.

SERENIA

Siren

Mysterious beings connected to the waters surrounding Silvermoon. Sirens can shift between aquatic and human forms, and their enchanted songs are said to influence both water and the minds of those who hear them.

KAELTHORN

Keraunos

Powerful thunder horses whose manes burn with magical fire and energy. Legends claim that their hooves can ignite the ground beneath them when they charge.

ELDERGARD

Gorgon

Ancient creatures strongly connected to earth magic. Their legendary gaze can turn living flesh to stone, making them among the most respected and feared creatures in the magical realm.

CAELORIA

Hippogriff

Proud creatures that rule the highest mountain peaks. Their enormous wings can channel powerful currents of wind and are said to stir storms across the skies.

FAMILIARS OF SILVERMOON

Magical Companions

Familiars are magical companions that may form a bond with witches and wizards, offering guidance, protection and companionship throughout their magical journey.

Not every student has a familiar, and no student is required to seek one. The strongest bonds are rarely chosen by the witch or wizard alone — sometimes, the familiar chooses them.

Cat

Observant, independent and deeply attuned to subtle magic.

Raven

Clever and curious, with an unusual talent for finding hidden things.

Owl

Patient and watchful, often guiding its companion through uncertainty.

Toad

Quiet and surprisingly perceptive, especially around potions and herbs.

Hare

Quick, alert and sensitive to changes in the magical world.

Dog

Loyal and protective, forming exceptionally strong bonds with its companion.

Seal

Playful and gentle, with a natural connection to water magic and a remarkable sense for the emotions of others.
